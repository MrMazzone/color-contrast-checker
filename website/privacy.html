Privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

The app

Color Contrast Checker runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it makes no network requests.

It saves your last colors, recent color pairs and settings (theme, sample text, eyedropper preference) in the app’s local preferences on your device so they are there next time. Uninstalling the app removes them.

The eyedropper reads screen pixels only while you are picking a color, and only to show the magnifier and return the color you choose. Screen images are never saved or sent anywhere. On macOS the system color sampler does this; on Linux, your desktop’s color picker.

Links in the app, such as this website or the WCAG documentation, open in your web browser, which follows its own privacy policy.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, and its fonts are served from this domain. GitHub may log standard request information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service; see the GitHub privacy statement.

The contrast checker on the home page runs in your browser and does not send your colors anywhere.
